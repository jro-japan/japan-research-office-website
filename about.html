ABOUT

A small independent, evidence-focused research office.

Japan Research Office is based in Japan and operated by Hiroaki Tsuboi. It provides defined-scope primary-source research for business decisions.

Our role

Japan remains a core specialization. JRO directly researches Japanese-language primary sources on companies, suppliers, markets, products and commercial developments.

When a defined business question requires evidence outside Japan, the scope may also include relevant official and public primary sources in other markets. JRO does not position itself as a global consulting firm; coverage follows the evidence required by the question.

Our role is to establish and organize evidence, identify what it supports and make unknowns explicit. Final legal, engineering, compliance, procurement, financial and other professional decisions remain with the client and its advisers.

Operating principles

Primary-source preference

Use authoritative underlying evidence where available.

Explicit uncertainty

Separate Confirmed Facts, Supported Inferences and Unknowns.

Email-only communication

Scoping, project communication and delivery are handled by email.
