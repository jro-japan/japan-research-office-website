About Japan Research Office

Independent research support for global teams that need reliable intelligence from Japan.

Our role

Japan Research Office is an independent research practice focused on Japanese market, industrial, policy, patent and business information. We convert fragmented source material into structured English-language intelligence.

Our work emphasizes source traceability, explicit uncertainty and deliverables that can be used directly in internal decision processes.

Research principles

  • Primary-source preference
  • Transparent uncertainty
  • Structured evidence
  • Commercial relevance
  • Confidential handling

Decision-ready intelligence from Japanese public, industry and patent sources for global business teams.
