Privacy Policy

Information collected

We may collect contact details, company information, inquiry content, correspondence and information necessary to provide research services.

Use of information

Information is used to respond to inquiries, provide services, manage contracts, maintain security and comply with applicable obligations.

Sharing and service providers

We do not sell personal information. Service providers may process information for hosting, email, inquiry handling, analytics and business operations under applicable safeguards and service settings.

Website measurement and security

Website security, analytics and error-monitoring tools may be used where configured. Where consent controls are presented, visitors can use them to manage optional measurement choices.

Operator and inquiries

Japan Research Office is operated by Hiroaki Tsuboi in Japan. The initial inquiry is handled by email. Please provide only the information necessary to describe your research question.

AI-assisted research and confidential information

AI may assist public-source research, translation and drafting. Do not include confidential or personal information beyond what is necessary in an initial inquiry. Before providing restricted material, ask JRO to confirm the applicable processing arrangements, permitted tools, retention and confidentiality requirements in writing. Do not assume a specific storage location, retention period or AI provider's training policy from this website.

Access and other privacy requests

Contact JRO to request information about processing, correction or other applicable privacy rights. Identify the relevant correspondence without including unnecessary sensitive details. Requirements may depend on the circumstances and applicable law.

Contact

Privacy questions may be sent to hiroaki@japanresearchoffice.com.
