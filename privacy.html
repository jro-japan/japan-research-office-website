Privacy Policy

How Japan Research Office handles contact, inquiry and service-related information.

Information collected

We may collect contact details, company information, inquiry content, correspondence and information necessary to provide research services.

Use of information

Information is used to respond to inquiries, provide services, manage contracts, maintain security and comply with applicable obligations.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Service providers may process information for hosting, email and business operations under appropriate safeguards.

Contact

Privacy questions may be sent to hiroaki@japanresearchoffice.com.

Website security, analytics and inquiry processing

We use Cloudflare Turnstile to protect forms from automated abuse. With your consent, we may use Google Analytics 4 for aggregate website measurement and Microsoft Clarity for session-behaviour analysis such as heatmaps, recordings, rage clicks and dead clicks. You can change these choices using the "Privacy settings" control on the website.

Website errors may be recorded through Sentry or our internal error endpoint with personal-data collection disabled where technically possible. Inquiry data may be processed through Netlify Forms, Google Workspace and Google Sheets, and HubSpot CRM for response, record-keeping and sales follow-up.

We do not intentionally use these tools for advertising personalisation. Provider availability, configuration and retention are subject to the applicable service settings and agreements.
