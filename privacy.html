Frequently asked questions

Key information about scope, methods, deliverables and commercial terms.

What makes Japan Research Office different?

We focus on converting Japanese-language public and industry sources into concise English outputs designed for business decisions.

Do you provide legal or investment advice?

No. Our work is informational research and does not constitute legal, financial or investment advice.

Can you customize the scope?

Yes. Geography, sector, companies, keywords, source types, frequency and deliverable format can be customized.

Are source links included?

Where available and legally accessible, we include source titles, dates and URLs.

How do you use AI?

AI supports collection, classification, translation and drafting. Human review is applied to source traceability, factual consistency and uncertainty labeling.

Can you sign an NDA?

Yes. NDA and confidentiality requirements can be discussed before project start.

What formats are available?

Typical formats include PDF, PowerPoint, Excel and CSV.
