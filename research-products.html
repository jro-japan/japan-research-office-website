Japan Tender Eligibility Pre-Screen

Understand the published participation requirements before committing resources to a Japanese public tender.

US$400 per customer-selected tender, subject to written scope confirmation and document availability.

Request scope confirmation by email

What you receive

  • English summary of the identified procurement.
  • Requirement-by-requirement review of published eligibility and registration conditions.
  • Official source references and comparison with customer-provided evidence.
  • Confirmed facts, evidence gaps, unresolved questions and research limitations.

Review our quality standards and research methodology.

How to order

  1. Email the tender URL or documents and your business question.
  2. Receive written confirmation of scope, deliverable, fee, delivery date and applicable payment terms before ordering.
  3. Approve the scope and arrange payment before research starts, unless otherwise agreed in writing.
  4. Receive the agreed deliverable electronically.

No telephone or video meeting is required.

What is not included

Tender discovery, legal advice, bid preparation, submission, certification of eligibility or guarantees of contract awards. Missing evidence remains explicitly unresolved.
