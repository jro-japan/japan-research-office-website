Questions, corrections and complaints

Email-based support for research engagements and public research materials.

Report an evidence issue

Identify the report ID and version, page or claim, the suspected error and supporting source. Distinguish a factual error from newly published information, a changed source or a request outside the agreed scope.

Raise a complaint

For delivery, scope, billing or service concerns, include the engagement reference, relevant correspondence and requested resolution. Send the request to the official email address. Do not send passwords, card details or unrelated confidential information.

Changes, cancellation and refunds

Before ordering, confirm the conditions for scope changes, additional fees, cancellation before or after work begins, correction requests and any refund in the written agreement.
