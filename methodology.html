METHODOLOGY

Research methodology

A transparent process for collecting, evaluating and delivering Japanese-source intelligence.

RESEARCH PROCESS

From scope to delivery

  1. 1

    Scope

    Define the business question, target entities, geography, date range and deliverables.

  2. 2

    Collect

    Prioritize official, primary and authoritative sources, supplemented where necessary.

  3. 3

    Verify

    Cross-check names, dates, figures, source context and duplicate records.

  4. 4

    Classify

    Apply categories, confidence labels, priority scores and structured metadata.

  5. 5

    Analyze

    Separate verified facts from inference, hypotheses and recommendations.

  6. 6

    Deliver

    Review scope, disclaimers, formatting, links and client-facing clarity.

RESEARCH STANDARDS

Clear about what is known—and what is not.

We distinguish sourced facts from analysis, preserve source traceability, and do not present unverified assumptions as facts.
