FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is JRO limited to Japan?

Japan is JRO's core specialization, particularly Japanese-language primary-source research. When the business question requires it, a defined scope may also include relevant official and public primary sources outside Japan.

What is an Evidence Check?

A focused research engagement that starts with a concrete question or claim and tests it against identified primary evidence. The output separates Confirmed Facts, Supported Inferences and Unknowns.

What is Commercial Change Intelligence?

Evidence-led research into commercially relevant state changes. It asks what changed, what primary evidence confirms and what remains unknown. It is not positioned as generic news monitoring.

Does JRO make legal, engineering, compliance or procurement decisions?

No. JRO provides research evidence and analysis. Final professional and business decisions remain with the client and its advisers.

Are source links included?

Where available and legally accessible, important evidence is presented with enough source information to support traceability.

How is uncertainty handled?

JRO does not fill an evidentiary gap with an unstated assumption. Material uncertainty is identified as an Unknown, while an inference is labelled and its evidentiary basis stated.

How do you use AI?

AI may support collection, classification, translation and drafting. The same source-traceability and evidence-classification standards apply to client-facing work.

How do we communicate?

JRO operates by email. You can send a research question and receive a proposed scope without booking a call or meeting.

How are fees and timing determined?

They depend on the defined scope, source coverage, complexity, deliverable and timing. These are agreed before the work starts.
