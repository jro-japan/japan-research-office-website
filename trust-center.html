Review JRO before engaging.

A starting point for procurement, finance, legal and management teams evaluating an independent research provider.

Who you are dealing with

Japan Research Office is an independent research practice operated by Hiroaki Tsuboi in Japan. Contact: hiroaki@japanresearchoffice.com. Communication is by email. See About JRO.

Before vendor onboarding or payment, request the legal contracting identity, billing details and any documentation your organization requires. Confirm availability and suitability before approving a purchase. This page is not a certificate of registration, accreditation or insurance.

Evidence you can inspect

Source traceability

Review the research method and quality controls, including explicit distinctions between facts, inferences and unknowns.

Actual source demonstration

Read a worked public-source example. It is not a client engagement, endorsement or certification.

AI use and limits

AI may assist collection, classification, translation and drafting. Evidence must support the claims. No assertion of AI-free work or independently certified review is made.

Vendor review checklist

Procurement
Confirm scope, deliverable, exclusions, timing and acceptance requirements in writing.
Finance
Confirm invoice requirements, contracting and beneficiary details, currency, payment method, timing and fees before payment.
Legal and information security
Review website terms and privacy information. Raise requirements for confidentiality, external AI processing, retention, usage rights and governing terms before sharing restricted information or ordering.
Management
Assess the research library, scope limits and whether the proposed deliverable answers the decision question.

Vendor documentation to request

Request confirmation of legal form, contracting name, billing address and tax documentation, if required. No registration number, tax status, standard settlement period or data-processing certification is represented here. Tax and payment treatment must be checked for the particular transaction.

Questions or concerns

Use the official email address to raise factual errors, delivery concerns or billing questions. Include the engagement reference, affected claim or document and relevant evidence.
