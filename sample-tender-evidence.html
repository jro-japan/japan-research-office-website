PUBLIC-SOURCE RESEARCH SAMPLE · JRO-SAMPLE-001 · v1.0

What does a Japanese library tender actually require?

Historical, limited-scope demonstration based on an official notice. Not a client engagement or an active sales lead.

Evidence date: 27 November 2026. Source publication: 29 July 2026. Scope: Notice-level review only; no bidder evidence or specifications obtained. The original submission deadline has passed.

Executive finding

The published notice describes a civilian book-digitization service. It sets supplier qualification, experience and documentation requirements. No particular company's eligibility can be established from the notice alone.

Confirmed facts

F1 · Scope

Approx. 450,000 image frames planned; performance deadline 30 March 2027. Source S1, sections 1(2) and 1(4).

F2 · Qualification

For the relevant FY2025–2027 qualification period, grade A or B in manufacture of goods or provision of services, with Kanto–Koshinetsu regional eligibility; the notice also allows qualification obtained by the evidence deadline. S1 §2(3).

F3 · Capability

Evidence of relevant experience, technical capability and equipment is required. S1 §2(4).

F4 · Procedure

Evidence submission: 18 September 2026, noon. Bid submission: 7 October 2026, noon. Japanese language and Japanese currency apply to the process. S1 §§3(4)–(5), 4(1).

Additional notice conditions

  • Other eligibility conditions include the statutory exclusions, absence of suspension and additional prescribed qualifications (S1 §§2(1), (2), (5), (6)).
  • Applicants without prior NDL digitization contracting experience must attend the briefing; the notice also requires telephone confirmation of the email request for electronic bid documents (S1 §§3(2), (3)).
  • Failure to meet eligibility or required obligations invalidates the bid under the notice (S1 §4(4)).

Supported inference

Under the original notice, a supplier unable to demonstrate the required qualification or capability would have a material eligibility gap. This is a research inference, not an authority's determination.

Unknowns and evidence needed

  • Bidder qualification certificate, grade, geographic coverage and validity: unknown.
  • Bidder experience, technical capability and supporting documents: unknown.
  • Full bid specifications, amendments, cancellation, award and subsequent status: not checked.

Screening conclusion: Not assessed for any supplier. No current opportunity, eligibility, award or revenue is implied.

Source and review notes

S1: National Diet Library, tender notice for digitization of domestically published books (practical books, part 2), 29 July 2026. Japanese official notice with English summary. Section references above refer to the Japanese notice. Source website checked on 27 November 2026; later changes have not been verified.
