QUALITY

Research quality is evidence discipline.

JRO focuses on traceability, factual consistency, explicit uncertainty and deliverables that make the underlying evidence clear and useful for business decisions.

Source quality

Primary and authoritative sources are preferred. Source identity, date, status and context are checked where relevant.

Claim control

Material claims are tied to evidence. Conflicting sources and unresolved gaps are surfaced rather than silently reconciled.

Evidence classification

Confirmed Facts, Supported Inferences and Unknowns are kept distinct.

Traceability

Important evidence is presented so the client can return to the underlying source where legally and technically accessible.

Scope control

Research is evaluated against the agreed question, geography, time period and deliverable rather than expanding without definition.

Delivery review

Names, dates, figures, links, wording, limitations and client-facing clarity are reviewed before delivery.

AI may assist the workflow; evidence standards do not change.

AI can support collection, classification, translation and drafting. JRO remains responsible for applying the stated research process and evidence labels to client-facing outputs.
