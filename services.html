SERVICES

Research built around the evidence a business question requires.

JRO organizes its work into three broad research categories so each engagement can be scoped around the client’s specific business question and the evidence required to answer it.

Japan Intelligence

Research on Japanese companies, suppliers, facilities, products, markets and commercial developments. Japanese-language primary sources are a core JRO specialization.

Typical questions include company and supplier research, market developments, product or facility facts, counterparties and Japan-specific commercial evidence.

Evidence Checks

Start with a concrete customer question and test it against primary evidence. Subjects may include a company, supplier, facility, product, standard, certification, failure, transaction, regulatory issue or document.

Outputs distinguish Confirmed Facts, Supported Inferences and Unknowns. JRO supplies evidence; the client's legal, engineering, compliance, procurement or other professional judgment remains separate.

Commercial Change Intelligence

Research into commercially relevant state changes identified in primary sources: regulatory actions, corporate changes, facility or manufacturing changes, transactions, divestitures, supplier developments, procurement, standards and certification changes.

The core questions are: What changed? What does the primary evidence confirm? What remains unknown?

COVERAGE

Japan first; broader primary-source coverage when required.

Japan remains a core specialization of Japan Research Office. Where the defined business question requires evidence outside Japan, JRO may research relevant official and public primary sources in other markets. This is question-led research coverage, not a claim to be a global consulting firm.

ENGAGEMENT

Defined scope before research starts.

Scope, sources, geography, time period, deliverable, fee and timing are agreed for the specific question. Work is handled by email.
