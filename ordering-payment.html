BEFORE YOU ORDER

From research question to delivery.

Review the proposed engagement and payment details before committing funds.

Ordering steps

  1. 1

    Send the question

    Email the relevant public URL or document reference, business objective, deadline and preferred output. Do not send confidential documents or payment credentials at this stage.

  2. 2

    Review the written proposal

    Confirm the research scope, exclusions, deliverable, fee, timing and applicable terms. Clarify the evidence gaps and conditions for starting work.

  3. 3

    Confirm procurement and payment

    Agree the order approval method and payment arrangements in writing. If your company requires a purchase order or invoice, request confirmation before committing.

  4. 4

    Research and review

    Research follows the agreed scope and the methodology and quality approach.

  5. 5

    Receive and review the deliverable

    Confirm electronic delivery format, route and acceptance conditions in the agreement. Raise questions or concerns by email.

Payment checklist

No online checkout is offered on this page. Before making payment, obtain written confirmation of:

  • Legal contracting identity and beneficiary details.
  • Available payment method and invoice or receipt requirements.
  • Currency, amount, applicable taxes and transfer charges.
  • Payment deadline, start conditions and any additional cost.

Verify any changed payment instructions through the established correspondence before transferring funds. Do not send card numbers, passwords or bank credentials by email.

Delivery, changes and questions

Agree the expected delivery date, language, file format, usage rights, handling of unavailable evidence, additional work, corrections, cancellation and refund conditions before ordering. No fixed turnaround or blanket refund policy is advertised here. Specific written terms govern each engagement.

Report an issue or request a change. Confirm any supplier onboarding and tax documents before ordering.
