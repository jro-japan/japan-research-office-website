About Japan Research Office

We help global teams use Japanese information more effectively.

Our role

Japan Research Office is an independent research practice focused on Japanese market, industrial, policy, patent and business information. We turn fragmented source material into structured, English-language intelligence.

Our work emphasizes source traceability, clear distinctions between fact and interpretation, and deliverables that can be used directly in internal decision processes.

Research principles

  • Primary-source preference
  • Transparent uncertainty
  • Structured evidence
  • Commercial relevance
  • Confidential handling

Decision-ready intelligence from Japanese public and industry sources for global business teams.
