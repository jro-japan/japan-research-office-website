Evidence discipline

  • Confirmed facts — directly supported by identified evidence
  • Supported inferences — reasoned conclusions with their basis stated
  • Unknowns — points the available evidence does not establish
  • Where the evidence stops, JRO says so.

METHOD

From business question to traceable evidence

  1. 1

    Define the question

    Set the decision context, entities, geography, time period and what needs to be established.

  2. 2

    Research primary sources

    Collect and cross-check relevant primary and authoritative sources, including Japanese-language material where applicable.

  3. 3

    Normalize the evidence

    Separate confirmed facts, supported inferences and unknowns, preserving source traceability.

  4. 4

    Deliver for the client's decision

    JRO provides evidence and analysis. Legal, engineering, compliance, procurement and other professional decisions remain with the client and its advisers.

EVIDENCE DISCIPLINE

We do not turn a signal into a conclusion the source cannot support.

A project does not prove procurement started. A tender does not prove a supplier was selected. An award does not identify an equipment OEM. A violation does not prove a purchase is required. A transaction does not prove supplier replacement. We state what the evidence confirms and what remains unknown.
